## Independent science evidence

[Yavuz & Yildirim 2020](https://www.jgeosci.org/content/jgeosci.303_Yavuz.pdf),
DOI 10.3190/jgeosci.303, section 2.1 (printed p74) and Table 4 SG1 (p83).
Downloaded PDF SHA256:
`a406a3a70a87b5b0448562ba75750b7dc491c119ce248b12664693764cbd3038`.
Pages were locally rendered and visually checked using the PDF skill. This
prevented using inconsistent Table 3 SG3 (MgO=0 but X-Mg>0) as an oracle.
The temporary renderer is QA-only, not a production dependency.

SG1 inputs: SiO2 36.7, Al2O3 20.9, FeO(total) 37.5, MgO 4.4, MnO .24,
CaO .45 wt.%; total 100.19, without normalization to 100.
Independent printed bulk APFU: Si 2.938, Al 1.972, Fe3 .153, Fe2 2.358,
Mg .525, Mn .016, Ca .039, tolerance .003 APFU for rounded published data.
Site expectations are hand allocations from those values, not generated by
the implementation: Z-Si 2.938, Z-Al .062, Y-Al 1.910, Y-Fe3 .153;
X total 2.938, Y total 2.063, Z total 3. The nonideal site sums are warned,
not forced to 3/2/3. Charge 24 and total cations 8 independently checked.

Independent ideal endmembers test pyrope/almandine/spessartine/grossular/
uvarovite/andradite; majorite and morimotoite test Y-Si and Y-Ti/Fe2.
Impossible Droop estimates, unsupported/censored/missing/mixed-Fe/wrong-unit
inputs reject the row rather than clamp or fabricate measurements.

## Use and remaining scope

Accept a garnet assignment in Minerals, press **Рассчитать формулу**. The
default is explicitly estimated Fe3 by Droop. **Настроить расчёт** offers all
Fe as Fe2 or independently measured FeO/Fe2O3; total Fe is not split measured
iron. Expand **Позиции X/Y/Z · оценка** only when needed. Save writes a separate
immutable result; it never rewrites source composition. A red error needs
source/assumption review, not an invented missing zero.

See [the implementation sequence](../product/STRUCTURAL_FORMULA_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).
Full Cpx/Opx T/M1/M2, mica/amphibole sites/anion models, feldspar, separate ore
methods, perovskite, nepheline, analcime and other feldspathoids remain future
versioned slices with independent published benchmarks. No method promoted
from draft in this slice.
